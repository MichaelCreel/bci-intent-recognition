I created an epoch quality score classifier using a random forest model. This classifier can be trained with multiple subjects, making its application more general and applicable for other subjects instead of being personally trained for a single subject. This classifier determines the quality of an epoch and marks epochs as good or bad. This can be used to filter out bad epochs from datasets, possibly improving the performance of models by using only quality training data.
